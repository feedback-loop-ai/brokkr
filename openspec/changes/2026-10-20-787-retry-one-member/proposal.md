# Change: a retry by member re-runs only the named panel members and carries the others' settled verdicts (#787)

Status: proposed. The law is decision 0093, proposed by this change; only the operator accepts it. The number is the commission's rule, the next after #802's, applied since #802 took 0092; it holds until the operator confirms it (S19).
Change: `2026-10-20-787-retry-one-member`.
Parent story: [#787](https://github.com/feedback-loop-ai/brokkr/issues/787), part of epic [#790](https://github.com/feedback-loop-ai/brokkr/issues/790) (cheaper recovery).

## Why

`panel_outcome` (`crates/brokkr-runtime/src/engine/panel.rs`) parks the whole attempt when any member of a panel is indeterminate, and `brokkr operator retry` re-runs the phase as a new attempt with every member. The verdicts that settled are discarded although nothing they judged has changed: the reviewed head, the working tree at it, the pinned bundle and each member's inputs are the same bytes. On 2026-10-19 one reviewer of a four-member panel ran 141 turns and wrote no verdict, and recovering it re-ran all four, about forty minutes for one stuck seat.

The pieces already exist. The journal records the head the protected phase was entered at (`phase/entered.head`, decision 0039) and the digest of every effect's requested input (`effect/requested.input_digest`), which the engine rebuilds and compares before it executes. The journal pins the run's manifest at `run/started`, and the manifest declares every seat's body, a panel's members by name. The engine already reads whether the operated tree is dirty for the ship gate (`git_dirty`), closed on a repository git cannot be spawned in and open on one git refuses to read: it reads what git prints and not how git exited, so a repository whose index is unreadable reports a clean tree, and so does a directory that is no repository, which the house's own machine proofs run over and ship from. `operator/commanded` carries an `args` object the fold never reads, which decision 0047 already uses for structured verb arguments. `effect/started` carries per-member entries keyed by the member's tag (`provenance`, decision 0016; `boundary`, decision 0046 ruling 3), and `effect/indeterminate` carries per-member evidence of what is not proven over (`unresolved_members`, #403). A sequence journals a `sequence-step-finished` marker for every step it finishes. A gate that moves the head already ends its attempt with a marked reason the fold decodes to its own park reason (`GATE-MOVED-HEAD`). The aggregate is a pure function of the members' result objects (`Aggregate::aggregate_results`). What is missing is a verb that names the members to run, a record of the verdicts that settled in an attempt that ended short of success and of the tree they judged, a rule that says when such a verdict may be carried, a tree reading that trusts git's exit and that each gate decides over for itself, and a readout that names a carried verdict and a refused carry.

## What Changes

- **A retry names the members that run** (decision 0093 ruling 1). `brokkr operator retry --member <name>`, repeatable, journals the members on its `operator/commanded` as `args.members`. A name is accepted bare, as the panel declares it, or as the tag the journal keys the member with, `<step>:<member>` in a sequence; it is journaled resolved, so `args.members` spells each member as `carried`, `settled_members` and the member markers spell it, and two names that resolve to one member are refused as that member given twice (S9). The fold does not read them; the engine reads them for the one effect the acceptance requests, and for every bounded attempt of that effect. A plain retry journals `args: {}` and re-runs every member, byte for byte as today. `--member` belongs to `retry` alone, and `--repo`, the operated repository the head and tree facts are read from, to the member form alone; the verb asks both before every refusal it makes today (S15), and `--repo` defaults as `resume`'s does, to the folder's root, else the current directory.
- **The carry applies to one panel** (ruling 2). The panel is read from the manifest the journal pins: the body it declares for the parked phase, under the case the phase's latest `phase/entered` records when the seat selects. The carry applies to the seat's body when it is a panel, or to a sequence's first step when that step is a panel and the parked attempt ended at it. A panel step after another step reads `context.prior_results`, which the carrying attempt builds again and the requested digest does not cover, so its verdicts judged a context no fact can hold still: a sequence whose first step is no panel but a later step is, is refused naming the panel step as following the first step, a fact of its own (S15). A sequence parked at a later step, the chief of a review sequence, is refused by name too when a member is unnamed, and re-running only the chief over every carried position is not offered here (S10). Names resolve against that one panel, which the manifest alone names: naming every member of it is the plain retry wherever the attempt ended (S18).
- **A settled verdict is carried only when nothing it judged has changed** (ruling 2). Four facts must hold: the reviewed head, read as the parked phase's latest `phase/entered.head` against the repository's head now; the working tree at it, read `clean` by the one tree reader (S16) at the two ends of the parked attempt and of every attempt a carried verdict is read from, as their terminal facts record it, and `clean` at the command and at the drive; the pinned bundle, held by the manifest gate `resume` applies before it builds any input; and the member's inputs, the seat input rebuilt for the source attempt's effect digesting to the `input_digest` that effect recorded. The verb checks the head, the tree and the journal's facts when the command is given and refuses with the reason, every observation at which the tree did not read `clean` named in one refusal, writing nothing. The engine checks all four before anything of the attempt spawns; a carry that no longer holds there starts the attempt on no driver and ends it `effect/indeterminate` with the marker `PANEL-CARRY-REFUSED` and its typed evidence, which names every source effect whose input no longer rebuilds as the tree refusal names every observation, so the run parks for the operator. Files the repository ignores are not reported by git and so not read by the carry: an edit to one is the accepted residual the ship gate accepts too (S12).
- **The tree is read once, by git's exit as well as by its words, and each gate decides over the reading** (ruling 2; S16). The one reader, `git_dirty`'s home, runs `git status --porcelain` and reads one of four readings: `clean`, `dirty`, `refused` (git exited other than zero) and `unrun` (git could not be run). Today it reads what git printed and counts only a failed spawn as dirty, so a directory that is no repository, an index git cannot read, an ownership refusal and a broken submodule, all exiting 128 with nothing printed, read clean. The carry and the panel attempt's `tree` record take the reading itself, and the carry needs `clean`. The ship gate's `dirty_worktrees` is decided over the reading and the head: `dirty` and `unrun` are dirty as today, `clean` is clean, and `refused` is dirty when the repository answers a head and clean when it answers none, so a directory that is no repository ships as it does today, the house's machine proofs over a bare directory included, and a repository with a commit that git refuses to read stops at `SHIP-DIRTY`, where today it reaches `done`. `ship_inputs` is not edited.
- **What is not carried refuses by name, in one order, before anything is written** (ruling 2; S15). The verb folds the run before it writes and asks, in this order: the arguments' shape, two facts, then the refusals it makes today; the run's state by the fold's one rule; the panel, two facts (the phase runs no panel; the panel step follows another step); the names, resolved against it and told apart; then, when every member is named, nothing more, and the attempt runs every member (S18); else the parked attempt's shape, four facts in order (the visit holds no attempt its panel ended; the attempt succeeded, and the run parked after it; it ended at a step after the panel; it records no verdict to carry); the unnamed members, every one that settled no verdict named in one refusal; the head; the tree, every observation that did not read `clean` named in one refusal, in the journal's order with now last (S12). The first fact that fails is the refusal reported, each fact has one text, and the text is the typed refusal's own, spoken by the verb and painted for a drive-time park alike. The parked attempt is the latest attempt of the phase's current visit, before the retry's own effect, that its panel ended: it succeeded, or its terminal fact records `tree`, which only the panel writes, or the panel step's `sequence-step-finished` marker stands. Every other attempt is passed over, one that started on no driver, one refused admission or provisioning, one the engine ended for a restart, for contention or for a refused held outcome, one a gate ended by moving the head, one a sequence's own checks on the panel step's aggregate ended, and one journaled before this change: none recorded what its panel judged, and the head and the tree it could have changed are read at the command and the drive (S11).
- **The carrying attempt aggregates as a fresh panel** (ruling 3). Only the named members run; every other member's verdict is carried from the attempt it settled in. The declared aggregate joins carried and fresh verdicts exactly as it joins a fresh panel's, so the result, its inputs and its notes are the bytes a full re-run produces on the same verdicts, and policy routes them unchanged: a residual or a finding from the retried member goes where the table sends it today. Every per-site record and plan of the carrying attempt covers the members that run and no other: `provenance`, `boundary`, the driver label, the session offers, the admission claim and the member markers. A fresh member that is indeterminate parks the attempt; one that fails fails it, and decision 0006's bounded retry of that effect runs the named members again with the same carry, read among the attempts before the effect's request.
- **The journal names every carried verdict, and every panel attempt records what settled in it** (ruling 4; S6, S17). `effect/started` carries `carried`: one entry per carried member with its tag and the attempt its verdict settled in. Every panel attempt whose outcome its panel ended short of success records `tree`, the reading at the attempt's start and at its end, and `settled_members`, the result of each member that settled in that attempt, fresh, never a verdict it only carried; an attempt in which no member settled records `tree` alone, and `tree`'s presence is the mark by which an attempt its panel ended is told from one the engine or a sequence's own checks ended in its place. So a parked attempt's verdicts to carry are read from its `settled_members` and its `carried` together: a member that settled there is carried from it, a member it carried is carried from the attempt its entry names, and the same member stuck twice is carried from the attempt before. The keys are the member's tag as the markers and `carried` spell it, `<member>` for a seat's panel and `<step>:<member>` for a panel step; `unresolved_members` keys the bare name today and is not changed (S12). The three fields and `args.members` are optional, absent-by-default extension fields at `event_schema: 1`, published as `contracts/panel-carry.v1.schema.json` under the amended rule in `contracts/README.md`: no new event type, no seat-record version, nothing the fold reads, and an attempt that carries nothing journals an `effect/started` byte-identical to today's. The member marker `panel-member-finished` is written only for members that ran. Decision 0082 ruling 1 rules when the file lands (S13).
- **Every readout names a carried verdict as carried, and a refused carry by its fact** (ruling 5; S14). `brokkr-view` derives the carried participant once: it stands at status `carried` and names the attempt its verdict came from. A carry refused at drive time parks through `effect/indeterminate` and `run/parked`, and both carry the one reason: the marker `PANEL-CARRY-REFUSED`, one space, and the refusal as compact JSON. The fold's `close` arm and its `park` arm decode those bytes through one codec in `brokkr-core` to a `ParkReason` carrying the typed refusal, so the state holds the fact whichever of the two events it folded last, and every readout paints the refusal's own text, the sentence the verb refuses with. `brokkr runs`, `inspect`, `seats`, `watch`, the TUI, `ui.html`, the fleet readouts, `compare`, muninn, the console and the journal timeline's rows paint both and derive nothing of their own.

### S1 — One rule, two observers

The carry rule is one pure function over typed facts: the parked attempt's members and each one's outcome, its settled verdicts, its carried entries and the tree it recorded, the phase's recorded head, the head and the tree observed, and the names given. The verb observes the journal and the repository and decides; the engine observes the same, and the rebuilt input digests besides, and decides with the same function (decision 0071 rulings 5 and 10: observing is split from deciding, and the rule has one home). The verb alone was refused because a head moved between the acceptance and the drive would then be carried, a fail-open. The engine alone was refused because the issue asks the command to refuse, and the operator would otherwise learn of the refusal two commands later. The inputs condition is the engine's alone: the verb compiles no bundle, the manifest gate stands before any input is built, and that condition can only differ at drive time. The verb reads the panel's members from the manifest the journal pins at `run/started`, which declares every seat's body: it needs no bundle on disk, and the engine's manifest gate proves the bundle it later runs is the one the names were resolved against.

### S2 — A carry refused at the command writes nothing

Decision 0047 ruling 2 is the precedent: a verb that refuses before it commands leaves the journal as it was, and the refusal is the operator's to read at the terminal. With `--member`, every refusal that can be known before writing is answered there, the fold's own conditions included, read by the one rule the fold reads (`acceptance_refusal`) and reported in its words: a member retry on a run that is not parked journals nothing, where the plain retry journals its command and its `operator/rejected`, because the plain retry writes the command first and disposes of it after. After a member retry's command is written, only the fold's race refusal (a lost fence) can follow, journaled as today. Journaling the refused member retry was weighed and set aside: `operator/rejected.reason` is a closed vocabulary every readout maps to one sentence, and a refusal that names a member or two heads does not fit a word. A later ruling can add the record if the console needs it.

### S3 — A carry refused at drive time parks through an indeterminate

The attempt starts on no driver (`driver: "none"`, as `refuse_changed_input` already does when the rebuilt input moved) and ends `effect/indeterminate` with a marked reason, as `GATE-MOVED-HEAD` marks a gate that moved the head, so the fold parks by its existing arm and nothing is auto-retried: decision 0006 keeps an indeterminate for the operator. Running the whole panel instead was refused as silent: it spends exactly what the operator asked not to spend. A `run/parked` written at `ExecuteEffect` with no attempt was refused: the relation admits it, but it leaves a requested effect with no terminal fact, which every reader of effects would have to learn. The marker's evidence is the same typed refusal the verb speaks, encoded as compact JSON after the marker and one space, as `MovedHead` is; the fold decodes it to its own park reason, and `run/parked` carries the same bytes in its `reason` so that the fold reads it back (S14).

### S4 — A carried verdict is named on `effect/started`, not on the member marker

The marker `panel-member-finished` is a closed seat record (`contracts/seat-record.v6.schema.json`, `additionalProperties: false`); naming the source there needs a seventh seat-record version for one field. `effect/started` already carries per-site entries keyed by the same tag, `provenance` and `boundary`, and the view reads them per member; `carried` is a third list of that shape. A carried member writes no marker in the carrying attempt and appears in none of its per-site records or plans: nothing of it ran, and a marker saying `succeeded` with no model, no session and no checkpoints, or a `provenance` entry naming a model served to an attempt it never ran in, would be a half-truth the readouts would then have to unlearn. Its evidence stands in its source attempt, which the `carried` entry names.

### S5 — The source is read from the parked attempt alone

A member's verdict is carried from the parked attempt when it settled there, its bytes in that attempt's `settled_members`, or from the attempt the parked attempt's own `carried` entry names, its bytes in that attempt's `settled_members`; so "the attempt it settled in" is exact and no search over the phase's attempts is needed. A member that settled in an earlier attempt, was named again and did not settle is not carried: the operator named it to judge again, and refusing by name is the safer reading. The parked attempt is the latest attempt of the parked phase's current visit that its panel ended (S11); an earlier visit judged an earlier head and is never a source.

### S6 — `settled_members` and `tree` ride both terminal facts short of success

A park reached by exhausted failed attempts (decision 0006) has a failed attempt as its latest, and its settled verdicts are as sound as an indeterminate attempt's; the record is one type written on both, by the panel whose outcome ends the attempt: a seat's panel body, or the panel step whose outcome ended a sequence. `tree` is written whenever that panel ran and the attempt ended short of success, whether or not a member settled, because the carry reads it at the parked attempt's two ends and a carrying attempt may settle nothing (S17); `settled_members` is written only when a member settled in that attempt, so an all-indeterminate panel records `tree` alone. Since only the panel writes `tree`, its presence is also the mark by which an attempt the panel ended is told from one the engine ended in its place, a refused admission or a restart (S11). A sequence ended by a later step records nothing for an earlier panel step, which already aggregated and whose verdicts the sequence no longer holds apart. Nor does a sequence whose own checks on the panel step's aggregate ended the attempt, the fourth pass's finding: after the panel's outcome succeeds and before the step's marker is journaled, `between_steps` fails the attempt on an aggregate outside the step's declared results, which `__member-schema-invalid__` is when a member's success carries no `result` string; the fence's refusal of the step's marker fails it; and `carry_change` would park it on a malformed `inputs.change`, which neither aggregate writes, so that arm is never reached from a panel step. Those terminal facts are the sequence's, written with the step's name, and record neither field: the record is the panel's, written where the panel's outcome is the attempt's, and a sequence's check on the aggregate is to a panel step what `decide`'s result check is to a seat's panel body. The seat-level twin settles it: the same malformed member in a seat's panel body reaches `effect/succeeded` with that word, `decide` parks the run by decision 0001's result check, and that park is refused by name as a succeeded attempt (S8, S15 item 5). Recording the sequence's case would admit by member in a sequence what a seat refuses, for the cost of threading the panel's readings into the sequence's own terminal facts; the attempt is passed over instead (S11), and the plain retry re-runs the panel. `effect/succeeded` records nothing new: a panel that succeeded has no park to be retried from, and a rule-driven park after a success re-runs the whole panel (S8). A panel step that is not the first records the pair like any other when it ends the attempt: the record says what the panel judged, and which panel a carry may read is the carry rule's to decide (S10), not the record's.

### S7 — The reviewed head is the phase's entry head

Decision 0039 records the protected phase's head at entry. A retry re-enters no phase, so the latest entry is the one every attempt of the visit judged, and the gate-head observation proves no gate moved it inside an attempt. The recorded head is checked against the contract's commit-id shape before git sees it, as `fixes_docs_only` checks it. A phase that recorded no head, a work panel's or a run with no repository, refuses the carry; recording a head for every phase entry is 0039's field and a ruling of its own. A head the repository cannot answer refuses the same way, and the tree is not asked after it.

### S8 — Left out, named

- A member retry through the Looper bridge and the console's Retry button: both stay the plain retry.
- Carrying a sequence's single step, or re-running only the chief of a review sequence over every carried position: a step-level carry is a later slice of epic #790. Naming the chief is refused as not a member; a sequence parked at the chief refuses `--member` as ended at a later step when a member is unnamed, and is the plain retry with its names journaled when every member is named (S10, S18).
- A panel step after another step (S10).
- A park after a succeeded panel attempt, by a rule such as `REVIEW-RESIDUAL-NONE` or by `decide`'s result check on the aggregate: nothing is recorded to carry, and the plain retry stands.
- A carry from an attempt a sequence's own checks ended at the panel step: passed over, as its seat-level twin is refused (S6, S11).
- A work panel in a phase that records no head: refused by name (S7).
- Carrying a member's model, session and checkpoints into the carrying attempt's row: the readouts name the source attempt instead.
- Carrying across phase visits.
- Journaling a refused member retry (S2).
- Edits made to the working tree and reverted inside one attempt's span, or in the span of an attempt that is passed over: unobserved, the residual the gate-head observation accepts (S12).
- Edits to files the repository ignores: unobserved by git and so by the carry, the residual the ship gate accepts (S12).
- A structured copy of the refusal on `run/parked.evidence`: the reason carries it (S14).
- A `tree` record on an attempt its panel did not end: one that started on no driver, one refused admission or provisioning, one the engine ended, a single seat's attempt or a sequence ended by a single step. Nothing judged the tree there, or nothing recorded what did (S11).

### S9 — Names
