# Change: a retry by member re-runs only the named panel members and carries the others' settled verdicts (#787)

Status: proposed. The law is decision 0092, proposed by this change; only the operator accepts it.
Change: `2026-10-20-787-retry-one-member`.
Parent story: [#787](https://github.com/feedback-loop-ai/brokkr/issues/787), part of epic [#790](https://github.com/feedback-loop-ai/brokkr/issues/790) (cheaper recovery).

## Why

`panel_outcome` (`crates/brokkr-runtime/src/engine/panel.rs`) parks the whole attempt when any member of a panel is indeterminate, and `brokkr operator retry` re-runs the phase as a new attempt with every member. The verdicts that settled are discarded although nothing they judged has changed: the reviewed head, the working tree at it, the pinned bundle and each member's inputs are the same bytes. On 2026-10-19 one reviewer of a four-member panel ran 141 turns and wrote no verdict, and recovering it re-ran all four, about forty minutes for one stuck seat.

The pieces already exist. The journal records the head the protected phase was entered at (`phase/entered.head`, decision 0039) and the digest of every effect's requested input (`effect/requested.input_digest`), which the engine rebuilds and compares before it executes. The engine already reads whether the operated tree is dirty, fail-closed, for the ship gate (`git_dirty`). `operator/commanded` carries an `args` object the fold never reads, which decision 0047 already uses for structured verb arguments. `effect/started` carries per-member entries keyed by the member's tag (`provenance`, decision 0016; `boundary`, decision 0046 ruling 3), and `effect/indeterminate` carries per-member evidence of what is not proven over (`unresolved_members`, #403). A gate that moves the head already ends its attempt with a marked reason the fold decodes to its own park reason (`GATE-MOVED-HEAD`). The aggregate is a pure function of the members' result objects (`Aggregate::aggregate_results`). What is missing is a verb that names the members to run, a record of the verdicts that settled in an attempt that ended short of success and of the tree they judged, a rule that says when such a verdict may be carried, and a readout that names it.

## What Changes

- **A retry names the members that run** (decision 0092 ruling 1). `brokkr operator retry --member <name>`, repeatable, journals the names on its `operator/commanded` as `args.members`. The fold does not read them; the engine reads them for the one effect the acceptance requests, and for every bounded attempt of that effect. A plain retry journals `args: {}` and re-runs every member, byte for byte as today. `--member` belongs to `retry` alone and names a member as the panel declares it; `--repo` names the operated repository the head and tree facts are read from, and belongs to the member form alone.
- **The carry applies to one panel** (ruling 2). The seat's body when it is a panel, or a sequence's first step when that step is a panel and the parked attempt ended at it. A panel step after another step reads `context.prior_results`, which the carrying attempt builds again and the requested digest does not cover, so its verdicts judged a context no fact can hold still: it is refused by name. A sequence parked at a later step, the chief of a review sequence, is refused by name too, and re-running only the chief over every carried position is not offered here (S10). Names resolve against that one panel.
- **A settled verdict is carried only when nothing it judged has changed** (ruling 2). Four facts must hold: the reviewed head, read as the parked phase's latest `phase/entered.head` against the repository's head now; the working tree at it, clean when the parked attempt started and ended, as its terminal fact records, and clean now; the pinned bundle, held by the manifest gate `resume` applies before it builds any input; and the member's inputs, the seat input rebuilt for the source attempt's effect digesting to the `input_digest` that effect recorded. The verb checks the head, the tree and the journal's facts when the command is given and refuses with the reason, writing nothing. The engine checks all four before anything of the attempt spawns; a carry that no longer holds there starts the attempt on no driver and ends it `effect/indeterminate` with the marker `PANEL-CARRY-REFUSED` and its typed evidence, so the run parks for the operator. Naming every member carries nothing, and no fact is checked.
- **What is not carried refuses by name, in one order, before anything is written** (ruling 2). The checks run against the state the verb folds before it writes: the run's state by the fold's one rule, the parked attempt's shape, the names, the head, the tree. The first that fails is the refusal reported, and each has one text. A member that never settled, indeterminate or failed, is not carried: left unnamed, the command refuses naming it. A name the panel does not have refuses naming the panel's members. A parked attempt that ran no panel, that succeeded, that a gate ended by moving the head, that ended at a later step, or whose terminal fact records no settled verdict refuses by that fact, and so does a journal written before this change. The parked attempt is the latest attempt of the phase's current visit that started on a driver: an attempt that started on none ran nothing and judged nothing, so a carry refused at drive time is not forfeited once the refusing fact is put right (S11).
- **The carrying attempt aggregates as a fresh panel** (ruling 3). Only the named members run; every other member's verdict is carried from the attempt it settled in. The declared aggregate joins carried and fresh verdicts exactly as it joins a fresh panel's, so the result, its inputs and its notes are the bytes a full re-run produces on the same verdicts, and policy routes them unchanged: a residual or a finding from the retried member goes where the table sends it today. Every per-site record and plan of the carrying attempt covers the members that run and no other: `provenance`, `boundary`, the driver label, the session offers, the admission claim and the member markers. A fresh member that is indeterminate parks the attempt; one that fails fails it, and decision 0006's bounded retry of that effect runs the named members again with the same carry.
- **The journal names every carried verdict** (ruling 4). `effect/started` carries `carried`: one entry per carried member with its tag and the attempt its verdict settled in. Every panel attempt that ends short of success records each settled member's result under `settled_members`, beside `tree`, the working tree's state when the attempt started and ended, on its `effect/indeterminate` or `effect/failed`. The keys are the member's tag as the markers and `carried` spell it, `<member>` for a seat's panel and `<step>:<member>` for a panel step; `unresolved_members` keys the bare name today and is not changed (S12). The three fields and `args.members` are optional, absent-by-default extension fields at `event_schema: 1`, published as `contracts/panel-carry.v1.schema.json` under the amended rule in `contracts/README.md`: no new event type, no seat-record version, nothing the fold reads, and an attempt that carries nothing journals byte-identical payloads. The member marker `panel-member-finished` is written only for members that ran. Decision 0082 ruling 1 rules when the file lands (S13).
- **Every readout names a carried verdict as carried, and a refused carry by its fact** (ruling 5). `brokkr-view` derives both once. The carried member's participant stands at status `carried` and names the attempt its verdict came from. The fold's `close` arm decodes `PANEL-CARRY-REFUSED` to its own park reason carrying the typed refusal, as it decodes `GATE-MOVED-HEAD`, and the view derives one sentence from it that names the fact that refused. `brokkr runs`, `inspect`, `seats`, `watch`, the TUI, `ui.html`, the fleet readouts and the console paint both and derive nothing of their own.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `panel-member-retry`: the verb and the carry rule: what a retry names, which panel the carry applies to, when a verdict is carried, what refuses by name and in what order, and how the carrying attempt runs and aggregates.
- `panel-attempt-record`: what the journal records of a panel attempt that ends short of success and of an attempt that carries, how every readout names a carried verdict, and how a carry refused at drive time is read.

### Modified Capabilities

- None. `operator-console-commands` keeps offering the plain retry; the console's Retry sends no members (see Not changed).

## Decisions

### S1 — One rule, two observers

The carry rule is one pure function over typed facts: the parked attempt's members and each one's outcome, its settled verdicts, its carried entries and the tree it recorded, the phase's recorded head, the head and the tree observed, and the names given. The verb observes the journal and the repository and decides; the engine observes the same, and the rebuilt input digests besides, and decides with the same function (decision 0071 rulings 5 and 10: observing is split from deciding, and the rule has one home). The verb alone was refused because a head moved between the acceptance and the drive would then be carried, a fail-open. The engine alone was refused because the issue asks the command to refuse, and the operator would otherwise learn of the refusal two commands later. The inputs condition is the engine's alone: the verb compiles no bundle, the manifest gate stands before any input is built, and that condition can only differ at drive time.

### S2 — A carry refused at the command writes nothing

Decision 0047 ruling 2 is the precedent: a verb that refuses before it commands leaves the journal as it was, and the refusal is the operator's to read at the terminal. With `--member`, every refusal that can be known before writing is answered there, the fold's own conditions included, read by the one rule the fold reads (`acceptance_refusal`) and reported in its words: a member retry on a run that is not parked journals nothing, where the plain retry journals its command and its `operator/rejected`, because the plain retry writes the command first and disposes of it after. After a member retry's command is written, only the fold's race refusal (a lost fence) can follow, journaled as today. Journaling the refused member retry was weighed and set aside: `operator/rejected.reason` is a closed vocabulary every readout maps to one sentence, and a refusal that names a member or two heads does not fit a word. A later ruling can add the record if the console needs it.

### S3 — A carry refused at drive time parks through an indeterminate

The attempt starts on no driver (`driver: "none"`, as `refuse_changed_input` already does when the rebuilt input moved) and ends `effect/indeterminate` with a marked reason, as `GATE-MOVED-HEAD` marks a gate that moved the head, so the fold parks by its existing arm and nothing is auto-retried: decision 0006 keeps an indeterminate for the operator. Running the whole panel instead was refused as silent: it spends exactly what the operator asked not to spend. A `run/parked` written at `ExecuteEffect` with no attempt was refused: the relation admits it, but it leaves a requested effect with no terminal fact, which every reader of effects would have to learn. The marker's evidence is the same typed refusal the verb speaks, encoded as compact JSON after the marker and one space, as `MovedHead` is; the fold decodes it to its own park reason (S14), and `run/parked.evidence` carries the decoded value as it carries the moved head.

### S4 — A carried verdict is named on `effect/started`, not on the member marker

The marker `panel-member-finished` is a closed seat record (`contracts/seat-record.v6.schema.json`, `additionalProperties: false`); naming the source there needs a seventh seat-record version for one field. `effect/started` already carries per-site entries keyed by the same tag, `provenance` and `boundary`, and the view reads them per member; `carried` is a third list of that shape. A carried member writes no marker in the carrying attempt and appears in none of its per-site records or plans: nothing of it ran, and a marker saying `succeeded` with no model, no session and no checkpoints, or a `provenance` entry naming a model served to an attempt it never ran in, would be a half-truth the readouts would then have to unlearn. Its evidence stands in its source attempt, which the `carried` entry names.

### S5 — The source is read from the parked attempt alone

A member's verdict is carried from the parked attempt when it settled there, or from the attempt the parked attempt's own `carried` entry names, so "the attempt it settled in" is exact and no search over the phase's attempts is needed: the verdict's bytes are read from the named attempt's `settled_members`. A member that settled in an earlier attempt, was named again and did not settle is not carried: the operator named it to judge again, and refusing by name is the safer reading. The parked attempt is the parked phase's latest attempt in its current visit that started on a driver (S11); an earlier visit judged an earlier head and is never a source.

### S6 — `settled_members` and `tree` ride both terminal facts short of success

A park reached by exhausted failed attempts (decision 0006) has a failed attempt as its latest, and its settled verdicts are as sound as an indeterminate attempt's; the record is one type written on both, by the panel whose outcome ends the attempt: a seat's panel body, or the panel step whose outcome ended a sequence. A sequence ended by a later step records nothing for an earlier panel step, which already aggregated and whose verdicts the sequence no longer holds apart. `effect/succeeded` records nothing new: a panel that succeeded has no park to be retried from, and a rule-driven park after a success re-runs the whole panel (S8). A panel step that is not the first records the pair like any other when it ends the attempt: the record says what the panel judged, and which panel a carry may read is the carry rule's to decide (S10), not the record's.

### S7 — The reviewed head is the phase's entry head

Decision 0039 records the protected phase's head at entry. A retry re-enters no phase, so the latest entry is the one every attempt of the visit judged, and the gate-head observation proves no gate moved it inside an attempt. The recorded head is checked against the contract's commit-id shape before git sees it, as `fixes_docs_only` checks it. A phase that recorded no head, a work panel's or a run with no repository, refuses the carry; recording a head for every phase entry is 0039's field and a ruling of its own. A head the repository cannot answer refuses the same way.

### S8 — Left out, named

- A member retry through the Looper bridge and the console's Retry button: both stay the plain retry.
- Carrying a sequence's single step, or re-running only the chief of a review sequence over every carried position: a step-level carry is a later slice of epic #790. Naming the chief is refused as not a member; a sequence parked at the chief refuses `--member` as ended at a later step (S10).
- A panel step after another step (S10).
- A park after a succeeded panel attempt (a rule-driven park such as `REVIEW-RESIDUAL-NONE`): nothing is recorded to carry, and the plain retry stands.
- A work panel in a phase that records no head: refused by name (S7).
- Carrying a member's model, session and checkpoints into the carrying attempt's row: the readouts name the source attempt instead.
- Carrying across phase visits.
- Journaling a refused member retry (S2).
- Edits made to the working tree and reverted inside one attempt's span: unobserved, the residual the gate-head observation accepts (S12).

### S9 — Names

`--member` takes the member's name as the panel declares it, resolved against the one panel the carry applies to (S10). In a sequence the journal's tag is `<step>:<member>`, and the tag spelled whole is accepted too. A name given twice is refused. A name that is a member of another panel step, or of no panel, is refused naming the eligible panel's members: with one eligible panel there is no bare name two steps can share.

### S10 — One eligible panel

The clarify judge found that a panel step after another step reads `context.prior_results`, the earlier steps' result objects built inside the attempt, and that the seat-input digest the inputs check compares does not cover them. A carried verdict there would be joined to a context it never judged while every fact held. Two settlements were weighed: carry only for a panel with no earlier step, or carry when every earlier step's result is carried and compared byte for byte. The second is a step-level carry and belongs to the epic's later slice; the first is what the shipped review sequences need, where the panel is the first step and the chief follows it. So the carry applies to a seat's panel body, or to a sequence's first step when that step is a panel and the parked attempt ended at it. A sequence parked at a later step refuses by name, since its earlier panel step already aggregated and no record holds its verdicts apart (S6). Both facts are refused at the verb and at drive time.

### S11 — An attempt that started on no driver is transparent

A carry refused at drive time ends an attempt that ran nothing. Were that attempt the one the next command reads, the carry would be forfeited by the refusal itself: the latest attempt records no settled verdict, and the operator who put the refusing fact right, a dirty tree cleaned or a house-rules edit reverted, could only re-run the whole panel. So the parked attempt is the latest attempt of the phase's current visit that started on a driver. An attempt started on none, a refused carry or a refused rebuilt input, is passed over: it judged nothing, and every identity fact is checked again at the new command and the new drive. An attempt that started on a driver and ended because a gate moved the head is not passed over: it judged a moving tree and is refused by name.

### S12 — The working tree is a fact, observed four times

The clarify judge found that uncommitted or untracked edits made after the park move none of the head, the bundle and the input digest, so a fresh member would judge bytes the carried members never saw. The issue's words are "the reviewed head", and the head was weighed as the identity on its own; it was refused, because the ship gate already refuses a dirty tree for the same reason and the engine already reads the fact fail-closed (`git_dirty`: `git status --porcelain`, an unreadable repository counting as dirty). The tree is observed when the parked attempt starts and when it ends, recorded on its terminal fact as `tree.at_start` and `tree.at_end`, and observed again at the command and at the drive; a carry needs all four clean. The record at the attempt's ends was added because edits present while the panel judged and reverted after it would otherwise pass every check: the carried verdicts judged the head and the edits, the fresh member the head alone. Edits made and reverted inside an attempt's span are unobserved, the same residual decision 0041 ruling 4's gate-head observation accepts at a gate's two ends. The keys of `settled_members` and `carried` follow the markers, `<step>:<member>` for a panel step, because that is the tag every per-member record of an attempt already carries; `unresolved_members` keys the bare name (#403) and is left as it is, recorded here as a difference, not changed, since its consumers read it today.

### S13 — The contract lands when the ruling that mints it is accepted

Decision 0082 ruling 1: a new contract version is minted only under an accepted ruling, a proposal's slice may build the version on a branch, and the version lands on `main` only once the ruling is accepted. `panel-carry.v1` is minted under 0092, so this change's pull request waits on the operator's acceptance of 0092, or the operator accepts it at the merge; the enforcement test 0082 names is unbuilt (#361), so the rule is carried by the pull request's own word until it is. Shipping the fields with no published schema was refused: the amended rule in `contracts/README.md` permits an additive field only when it is published as a numbered extension schema.

### S14 — A refused carry is read as its own park reason

The clarify judge found that a drive-time refusal would reach every readout as `effect <id> indeterminate: PANEL-CARRY-REFUSED <json>`, raw JSON through `ParkReason::Stated`, where `GATE-MOVED-HEAD` has its own decoded reason. Accepting the raw text was refused: an operator-facing readout leads with the fact, not with a marker and its evidence. The fold's `close` arm decodes the marker to a `ParkReason` variant carrying the typed refusal, through a codec in `brokkr-core` beside `gate_moved_head` (decision 0071 ruling 3: parsed once, at the edge), and `brokkr-view` derives one sentence from the typed value (ruling 7: one derivation) that names the fact that refused, the two heads or the dirty tree or the effect whose input moved. Every readout paints that sentence; the journal keeps the marker and its evidence, and `run/parked.evidence` carries the decoded value as it carries the moved head.

### S15 — Refusals in one order, each with one text

Every refusal text is pinned once, so the tests fix the order in which the verb asks. The order is the order of the facts' cost and certainty: the arguments' shape, which clap answers before anything runs; the run's state by the fold's one rule; the parked attempt's shape, read from the journal; the names, read against the panel the manifest and the parked attempt describe; the head, which reads git; the tree, which reads git again. The first check that fails is the refusal reported, and the later checks are not asked. The engine asks the same checks at drive time in the same order, and the inputs check last, and the first that fails is the marker's evidence.
