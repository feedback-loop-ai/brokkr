# Change: a retry by member re-runs only the named panel members and carries the others' settled verdicts (#787)

Status: proposed. The law is decision 0093, proposed by this change; only the operator accepts it. The number is the commission's rule, the next after #802's, applied since #802 took 0092; it holds until the operator confirms it (S19).
Change: `2026-10-20-787-retry-one-member`.
Parent story: [#787](https://github.com/feedback-loop-ai/brokkr/issues/787), part of epic [#790](https://github.com/feedback-loop-ai/brokkr/issues/790) (cheaper recovery).

## Why

`panel_outcome` (`crates/brokkr-runtime/src/engine/panel.rs`) parks the whole attempt when any member of a panel is indeterminate, and `brokkr operator retry` re-runs the phase as a new attempt with every member. The verdicts that settled are discarded although nothing they judged has changed: the reviewed head, the working tree at it, the pinned bundle and each member's inputs are the same bytes. On 2026-10-19 one reviewer of a four-member panel ran 141 turns and wrote no verdict, and recovering it re-ran all four, about forty minutes for one stuck seat.

The pieces already exist. The engine reads the repository's head (`git_head`) and records it, in the commit-id shape `phase-entered-head.v1` pins, where the protected phase is entered (`phase/entered.head`, decision 0039); the journal records the digest of every effect's requested input (`effect/requested.input_digest`), which the engine rebuilds and compares before it executes. The journal pins the run's manifest at `run/started`, and `brokkr resume` compiles the recipe again and refuses it unless it reproduces that pin (`compile_from_manifest`, then the comparison that refuses `ManifestMismatch`), so a bundle compiled under that gate holds every seat's body as the run ran it, a panel's members by name. The engine already reads whether the operated tree is dirty for the ship gate (`git_dirty`), closed on a repository git cannot be spawned in and open on one git refuses to read: it reads what git prints and not how git exited, so a repository whose index is unreadable reports a clean tree, and so does a directory that is no repository, which the house's own machine proofs run over and ship from. `operator/commanded` carries an `args` object the fold never reads, which decision 0047 already uses for structured verb arguments. `effect/started` carries per-member entries keyed by the member's tag (`provenance`, decision 0016; `boundary`, decision 0046 ruling 3), and `effect/indeterminate` carries per-member evidence of what is not proven over (`unresolved_members`, #403). A sequence journals a `sequence-step-finished` marker for every step it finishes. A gate that moves the head already ends its attempt with a marked reason the fold decodes to its own park reason (`GATE-MOVED-HEAD`). The aggregate is a pure function of the members' result values (`Aggregate::aggregate_results`). What is missing is a verb that names the members to run, a record of the verdicts that settled in an attempt that ended short of success and of the head and the tree they judged, at both ends of the attempt, a rule that says when such a verdict may be carried, a tree reading that trusts git's exit, and a readout that names a carried verdict.

## What Changes

- **A retry names the members that run** (decision 0093 ruling 1). `brokkr operator retry --member <tag>`, repeatable, journals the members on its `operator/commanded` as `args.members`. A member is named by its tag, the one spelling the journal keys it with and every readout labels it by: `<member>` in a seat's panel and `<step>:<member>` in a panel step. Each name is matched exactly against the panel's tags and read no other way, so no name can read as two members; a name that is no member's tag is refused naming the panel's tags in declared order, and a tag given twice is refused naming it (S9, S27). `args.members` spells each member as `carried`, `settled_members` and the member markers spell it. The fold does not read them; the engine reads them for the one effect the acceptance requests, and for every bounded attempt of that effect. A plain retry journals `args: {}` and re-runs every member, byte for byte as today. `--member` belongs to `retry` alone, and four of `resume`'s arguments to the member form alone: `--repo`, the operated repository the head and tree facts are read from and the bundle is compiled for, and `--bundle`, `--recipe` and `--recipes-dir`, which find the recipe the verb compiles (S1). The verb asks whether `--member` and those four stand where they belong before every refusal it makes today (S15), and each of the four is read and defaulted as `resume` reads it: `--repo` to the folder's root, else the current directory, and the recipe to the found folder's bundle when neither `--bundle` nor `--recipe` is typed.
- **The carry applies to one panel** (ruling 2). The panel is read from the pinned bundle, which the verb compiles as `resume` does and holds to the manifest the journal pins at `run/started`, a compile that gate refuses being refused in `resume`'s own words before any panel fact (S1, S15): the body the bundle holds for the parked phase, selected as the engine selects a body for an effect of that phase, under the strategy the folded state holds. The verb holds that bundle as `brokkr resume` holds it: it builds the engine through `resume`'s own admission and spawns and drives nothing (S27). The carry applies to the seat's body when it is a panel, or to a sequence's first step when that step is a panel and the parked attempt ended at it. A panel step after another step reads `context.prior_results`, which the carrying attempt builds again and the requested digest does not cover, so its verdicts judged a context no fact can hold still: a sequence whose first step is no panel but a later step is, is refused naming the panel step as following the first step, a fact of its own (S15). A sequence parked at a later step, the chief of a review sequence, is refused by name too when a member is unnamed, and re-running only the chief over every carried position is not offered here (S10). Names are matched against that one panel's tags, which the compiled bundle alone names: naming every member of it is the plain retry wherever the attempt ended (S18).
- **A settled verdict is carried only when nothing it judged has changed** (ruling 2). Four facts must hold: the reviewed head, recorded at the two ends of the parked attempt and of every attempt a carried verdict is read from, as their terminal facts record it, each end equal to the repository's head now, at the command and at the drive (S7); the working tree at it, read `clean` by the one tree reader (S16) at the two ends of the parked attempt and of every attempt a carried verdict is read from, as their terminal facts record it, and `clean` at the command and at the drive; the pinned bundle, held by the manifest gate `resume` applies before it builds any input, which the verb applies too before it reads the panel; and the member's inputs, the seat input rebuilt for the source attempt's effect digesting to the `input_digest` that effect recorded. The verb asks all four facts when the command is given, the inputs through the engine `brokkr resume` builds, rebuilt by the one function the drive rebuilds them with (S27), and refuses with the reason, writing nothing: every recorded end whose head is not the head now is named in one refusal, every observation at which the tree did not read `clean` in another, and every source effect whose input no longer rebuilds to its digest in a third. The engine asks the same four before anything of the attempt spawns; a carry that no longer holds there starts the attempt on no driver and ends it `effect/indeterminate` with the refusal's own sentence as its reason, the sentence the verb refuses the same fact with, so the run parks for the operator as a stated indeterminate (S14). Files the repository ignores are not reported by git and so not read by the carry: an edit to one is the accepted residual the ship gate accepts too (S12).
- **The tree is read once, by git's exit as well as by its words, and the carry needs `clean`** (ruling 6; S16). The one reader, which `git_dirty` decides over, runs `git status --porcelain` and reads one of four readings: `clean` (git exited zero and printed nothing), `dirty` (git printed something, whatever its exit), `refused` (git exited other than zero and printed nothing) and `unrun` (git could not be run). Today `git_dirty` reads what git printed and counts only a failed spawn as dirty, so a directory that is no repository, an index git cannot read, an ownership refusal and a broken submodule, all exiting 128 with nothing printed, read clean to it. The carry and the panel attempt's `tree` record take the reading itself, and the carry needs `clean`. The ship gate's `dirty_worktrees` is `git_dirty`'s decision over the reading alone, `true` on `dirty` and `unrun` and `false` on `clean` and `refused`, which is today's answer byte for byte: the ship gate's semantics do not change in this change, and the hole its `refused` reading leaves, a repository with a commit whose index git cannot read shipping as clean, is older than this change and is named for a decision of its own (S16, S22). `ship_inputs` is not edited.
- **What is not carried refuses by name, in one order, before anything is written** (ruling 2; S15). The verb folds the run before it writes and asks, in this order: the arguments' shape, two facts, then the refusals it makes today; the run's state by the fold's one rule; the visit's records, read once into typed records, a field that does not read refusing by the attempt and the field (S22, S23); the bundle and the panel, three facts (the engine `resume` builds admits the run, the pinned bundle compiled and held to the manifest and the charters the journal pins, refused in `resume`'s words; the phase runs no panel; the panel step follows another step); the names, two facts, each matched exactly against the panel's tags; then, when every member is named, nothing more, and the attempt runs every member (S18); else the parked attempt's shape, four facts in order (the visit holds no attempt its panel ended; the attempt succeeded, and the run parked after it; it ended at a step after the panel; it records no verdict to carry); the unnamed members, every one that settled no verdict named in one refusal; the head, every attempt end whose recorded head is not the head now named in one refusal, in the journal's order with now last (S7); the tree, every observation that did not read `clean` named in one refusal, in the journal's order with now last (S12); the inputs, every source effect whose seat input, rebuilt under that effect's own id, no longer digests to its `effect/requested.input_digest`, named once in one refusal, in the order the carried entries first name them (S27). The first fact that fails is the refusal reported, each fact has one text, and the text is the typed refusal's own, spoken by the verb and painted for a drive-time park alike, `resume`'s admission excepted, which is `resume`'s own and asked by `resume` before any drive. The parked attempt is the latest attempt of the phase's current visit, before the retry's own effect, that its panel ended, and a three-way test alone decides it: the attempt succeeded, or its terminal fact records `tree`, which only the panel writes, or the panel step's `sequence-step-finished` marker stands. An attempt that bears any of the three marks is never passed over, whatever ended it after its panel: a sequence's attempt whose panel step's marker stands and which a later step, a restart, contention, a refused held outcome or a gate moving the head then ended, or which was journaled before this change, is the parked attempt, and the shape fact "it ended at a step after the panel" refuses it naming the step it ended at. Only an attempt that bears none of the three is passed over: one that started on no driver; one refused admission or provisioning; one the engine ended for a restart, for contention or for a refused held outcome before its panel ended; one a gate ended by moving the head before its panel ended, its `GATE-MOVED-HEAD` written in the place of the panel's terminal fact or before the panel step's marker; one a sequence's own checks on the panel step's aggregate ended before the step's marker; and one journaled before this change that neither succeeded nor journaled the panel step's marker. None of these recorded what its panel judged, and the head and the tree it could have changed are read at the command and the drive (S11).
- **The carrying attempt aggregates as a fresh panel** (ruling 3). Only the named members run; every other member's verdict is carried from the attempt it settled in. The declared aggregate joins carried and fresh verdicts exactly as it joins a fresh panel's, so the result, its inputs and its notes are the bytes a full re-run produces on the same verdicts, and policy routes them unchanged: a residual or a finding from the retried member goes where the table sends it today. Every per-site record and plan of the carrying attempt covers the members that run and no other: `provenance`, `boundary`, the driver label, the session offers, the admission claim and the member markers. A fresh member that is indeterminate parks the attempt; one that fails fails it, and decision 0006's bounded retry of that effect runs the named members again with the same carry, read among the attempts before the effect's request.
- **The journal names every carried verdict, and every panel attempt records what settled in it** (ruling 4; S6, S17). `effect/started` carries `carried`: one entry per carried member with its tag and the attempt its verdict settled in. Every panel attempt whose outcome its panel ended short of success records `tree`, the reading at the panel's start, before any member of it spawned, and at its end, before its terminal fact, which for a seat's panel and a sequence's first step are the attempt's own two ends and for a later panel step the step's (S28); `head`, the repository's head at those same two moments, read by `git_head` in the commit-id shape `phase-entered-head.v1` pins, each end absent where the repository answered no head; and `settled_members`, the result of each member that settled in that attempt, fresh, never a verdict it only carried. An attempt in which no member settled records `tree` and `head` alone, and `tree`'s presence is one of the three marks by which an attempt its panel ended is told from one the engine or a sequence's own checks ended in its place, beside `effect/succeeded` and the panel step's `sequence-step-finished` marker (S11). So a parked attempt's verdicts to carry are read from its `settled_members` and its `carried` together: a member that settled there is carried from it, a member it carried is carried from the attempt its entry names, and the same member stuck twice is carried from the attempt before. The keys are the member's tag as the markers and `carried` spell it, `<member>` for a seat's panel and `<step>:<member>` for a panel step; `unresolved_members` keys the bare name today and is not changed (S12). The four fields and `args.members` are optional, absent-by-default extension fields at `event_schema: 1`, published as `contracts/panel-carry.v1.schema.json` under the amended rule in `contracts/README.md`: no new event type, no seat-record version, nothing the fold reads, and an attempt that carries nothing journals an `effect/started` byte-identical to today's. The member marker `panel-member-finished` is written only for members that ran. Decision 0082 ruling 1 rules when the file lands (S13).
- **Every readout that paints participants names a carried verdict as carried, and a refused carry by its sentence** (ruling 5; S14, S24). `brokkr-view` derives the carried participant once: it stands at status `carried` and names the attempt its verdict came from, by its id and by its place among the phase's attempts, and the surfaces that paint participants, `inspect`, `seats`, `watch`, the TUI, `ui.html` and the console, paint it; `brokkr runs` and the fleet readouts, whose run rows hold no participant, show nothing of a carried verdict. A carry refused at drive time parks as any stated indeterminate does: the `effect/indeterminate` carries, as its `reason`, the refusal's own sentence, the text the verb refuses with, and the fold's `close` arm reads it to the stated park reason it reads every unmarked reason to, `effect <id> indeterminate: <sentence>`, which `run/parked` then carries as today. No marker, no codec and no new `ParkReason` variant: `brokkr runs`, `inspect`, `seats`, `watch`, the TUI, `ui.html`, the fleet readouts, `compare`, muninn, the console and the journal timeline's rows paint the stated reason as they paint every stated reason today, and derive nothing of their own.

### S7 — The reviewed head is recorded at both ends of every panel attempt

The analyze pass found the shape of a present end unsettled between this record and S22's addition: settled that `panel-carry.v1` pins each end to the commit-id shape, that the writer stamps `git_head`'s answer only in that shape, reading an answer outside it as no head as `phase-entered-head.v1` reads one, and that a present end outside the shape is a record that does not read, refused naming the attempt and the field before any identity fact, never read as a head that recorded none (S23).
