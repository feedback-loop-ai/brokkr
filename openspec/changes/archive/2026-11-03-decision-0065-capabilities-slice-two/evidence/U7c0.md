## What changed in production

Only the row's three production files moved, and no new production file
was added.

`capabilities/binding.rs` is the comparison's one home and now its seal.
`Authority::admitted` is MB4's comparison of the dialect's typed egress
against the bundle's binding minimum, moved out of `carried`'s match arm
unchanged in its cause: below the minimum it returns `Unserved::Below`; at
or above it returns `Admitted`, which holds the protocol's own `Network`
and `Clearance` rather than runtime copies. The network is the closed
projection `network(egress)`: `local` is `Isolated`, `contracted` and
`uncontracted` are `Shared`, matched exhaustively. The receipt's `dialect`
is the dialect's sha256, and its `policy` is
`sha256_bytes(to_bytes(minimum word))`, the canonical bytes of exactly the
`EgressClass` minimum the comparison read (operator ruling 2026-10-15).
`Retention::effective` is `pub(crate)`, its rule unchanged. `Holding`
moved here from `capabilities.rs`, beside the facts it now carries, and
gained `pub admitted: Option<Admitted>`: the comparison's seal on an
admitted `mcp` holding, `None` for a native holding, which MB4 never
judges.

The seal sits on the resolver's production path (review return M1 of
this run). The front of `Authority::holding` — the grant reaching the
office, the loaded dialect, the admitted tools, GP1's gates and the
binding's reading — moved unchanged in order into a private
`Authority::sealed(site, capability) -> Result<Holding, Unheld>` in this
file, which builds the one production `Holding` and assigns `admitted`
from what `carried` returned. `carried` now returns
`Result<Option<Admitted>, Unserved>`: `None` for a native binding, the
SC4 and SC1 causes as before, and for an executable `mcp` binding
`self.admitted(dialect).map(Some)`, MB4's cause below the minimum. The
fence's own words moved from `carried` onto the sealed holding:
`Holding::native(realm, capability)` gives a native holding's provider
and adapter key from its `Implementation` and refuses an `mcp` holding
with `Unbound::Mcp`, the same text and the same incompatibility as
before, so every refusal the resolver and the compile give is unchanged
(the binding, dialect-policy and fence suites pass unedited) and nothing
is held through `mcp` until U9b. `Unserved::unheld` and `unbuilt` take
the dialect's name rather than the whole dialect, which is all they read.
321 to 439 lines.

`capabilities.rs` re-exports `Admitted` and `Holding` through its existing
`pub use binding::{…}` line. `holding()` opens with `self.sealed(..)?`,
asks the holding for its native pair, and keeps the provider, native-tool,
switch and restriction checks in their old order, now reading the tools
and restrictions from the sealed holding; it returns that holding. 1,995
to 1,955 lines, below its frozen baseline, which is lowered to 1,955.

`brokkr-protocol/src/broker.rs`: the `records!` macro derives `Serialize`
(and `PartialEq`, `Eq`) on every record's public struct, carrying each
field's `#[serde(..)]` from the same macro input the hidden `Fields`
decoder reads, so a wire name such as `Plan.intent`'s `box` is written
once. `Owner`'s derive list drops the `Serialize` (and `PartialEq`, `Eq`)
it had, because the macro now derives the same impls for every record; its
wire form is unchanged and pinned below. `Clearance` also derives `Clone`,
which `Holding`'s derived `Clone` needs. `Tree`'s tag and words moved from
a function-local enum into one private module-level `Kind<P>`, read into an
owned root by `Deserialize` (still through `Object`) and written from a
borrowed root by the new `impl Serialize for Tree`. `Privilege` and
`Network` derive `Serialize`/`PartialEq`/`Eq` beside their existing
`Deserialize`. `Inventory` gains no field. 325 to 345 lines.
