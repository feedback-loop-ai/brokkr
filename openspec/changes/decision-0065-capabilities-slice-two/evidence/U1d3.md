## What a launch is served now

`Isolated::resumes` in `brokkr-protocol/src/adapters/mcp.rs` declines a rejoin
when the launch's replacement is measured but its resume shape is not. It then
serves the cold replacement that `isolated` already admitted. This is SI2's
scenario: "a resume without measured isolation refuses that shape or follows
the existing cold replacement policy; it cannot silently use an unisolated
resume". The runtime reads only `ambient` for this, so H1's three axes move no
launch. On Linux:

- **A Codex work seat with harness hands** (workspace-write under the
  `harness` boundary) is no longer rejoined. Its SI2 record holds only resume
  shapes declared under its own hands (`bundle/mcp.rs`). The shipped
  `work-site` resume is declared under hands `none`, and U0b measured no
  rejoin with hands (X20). The offer is declined, and the seat is served its
  isolated cold command, OFF included. The inline Codex work seat (hands
  `none`) still rejoins on its measured ambient exclusion.
- **An unboxed Claude or LaneTally replacement** carries the strict empty
  set, as a cold launch already did. Main performs no Claude or LaneTally
  rejoin (both resume shapes are disabled or unmeasured).
- **A dsh seat** is served as on main. Its replacement is unmeasured, as it
  was when absent, so a lifted fence still refuses it at `replacement`.

On macOS no shape is measured. The fence serves every shape as before, and
the harness-hands Codex seat still rejoins (I1).
