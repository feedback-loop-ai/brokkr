## What was built

Every driver an attempt spawns keeps its own settlement record in the
attempt's entry of the protected reservation: the private
`settlement::Driver`, holding the site label it was spawned for, the
journal head U8d's `begun` took before it spawned (none where the site was
sealed no broker) and a `Witnessed` cleanup. `admission::Reserved` gains
`drivers`, defaulted and skipped when empty, so a reservation written
before U8e2 reads as one with no record.

The record is written Settling at the common dispatch door: `spawn_site`
(`engine/dispatch.rs`) takes an optional `Door` (lease, site label, the
driver's `Begun`) and, after every existing check and before
`DriverProcess::spawn`, calls `settlement::spawning`, which appends the
record under the admission lock. A record that cannot be sealed, or whose
directory is not proven synced, refuses the driver. Ordinary seats and
sequence steps reach the door through `run_driver`; panel members through
`MemberRun::report`, which `run_panel` hands each member's own head. The
record is sealed Settled by `settlement::ended`, only on
`Cleanup::Settled`, before the fold reads that driver's ledgers:
`Engine::folded` calls it for a single driver, and `settle_members` for
every member in its witness loop before any member is folded through
`Engine::settled_by`. No process identity is recorded and no scanner
starts (D6).

At restart, the `Cursor::EffectInFlight` arm in `engine.rs` calls
`Engine::restarted` (`engine/broker/fold.rs`). It takes the lease again
through `Engine::readopted` and `settlement::adopted`, which re-read the
run's reservation under the admission lock in the observed coordination
root, and adopt the attempt only where the reservation is this worktree's
and this run's, the attempt stands and was not witnessed Unresolved, and it
recorded at least one driver, every one Settled. Adoption reserves nothing
and makes no run directory or reservation; observing the root goes through
admission's own `Root::of`, which, as for any dispatch, makes the
coordination root itself where it is missing (INFO of run 3, corrected).
Missing proof gets today's judgment word for word: the restart's
`effect/indeterminate` reason, nothing read, the reservation standing. A
failed adoption is handed up, typed, with nothing journaled.

A recovered attempt folds each recorded driver from its recorded head
through U8d's own path. `Engine::tally`, which the live fold and recovery
share, reads the brokers through one `plans` step over the existing
`artifacts::provisioned` and `artifacts::pinned` readers and the existing
`ledger` and `expected`: the live fold names its brokers by its spawn's
sealed list, recovery by the plans the attempt's inventory pins whose owner
is the record's site. Both now append through one seam, `fold::confirmed`
(H1 below): `checkpoints::confirmed`, which appended with `append_chain` at
whatever head stood, is gone. No second fold, ledger reader, inventory
reader or plan reader was added. The restart's judgment is
`fold::restarted`, and `Engine::settle` then runs as for any ended attempt.
Nothing is spawned, provisioned again or replayed; operator conclude
(`engine/operator.rs`, untouched) keeps its own judgment and folds nothing.

## Assumptions and residuals

1. **The lease is taken again from the standing reservation.** The ruling
   says "through the existing admission path". This is read as under the
   admission lock, from the run's standing reservation, through
   admission's own `locked`, `read`, `sealed` and `reserved_in`. Nothing
   new is reserved and no run directory is made; the coordination root is
   observed through `Root::of`, which makes the root where it is missing,
   as every dispatch does. A fresh `admit` claim would re-seal the
   attempt's entry and drop its records; nothing spawns at restart, so no
   reach is compared again.
2. **The artifact root's identity is taken at the restart** (INFO).
   Admission's device and inode are not durable; a root that cannot be
   identified now fails the adoption, and nothing is sealed.
3. **The restarted lease counts as evidence at stake** (INFO). `staked` is
   not durable either, so a seal it cannot prove fails the run.
4. **Adoption seals the settlement fact early** (INFO). It is sealed at
   adoption, before the terminal event, because the records prove it and
   the door needs a durable mark to refuse. Another admission's recovery
   still releases the attempt only on a certified terminal event, so an
   adoption that dies or is refused before its end keeps the reservation
   standing.
5. **Two engines may both fold one attempt.** A running engine's last
   record is sealed before its own fold, so a restarted engine may adopt
   while it folds (H1). The journal is where this is closed: each call
   lands once, nothing lands after an end, and the engine that loses the
   race is refused `JournalMoved`, appending neither calls nor its own
   terminal. Under interleaving both may refuse, leaving the attempt in
   flight for a later restart; that is fail-closed. The running engine's
   own terminal racing a peer that appended nothing of its calls is
   #609's.
6. **What counts as absent proof.** A run directory that is missing, no
   attempt entry, no record, or a Settling or Unresolved driver; and a
   root `Root::of` does not return or a HOME the host does not report.
   That last pair is not only absence: a failed observation of a root that
   was protected when the attempt was admitted reads the same, because
   `observation.rs` and `root.rs` return no typed failure (L1 of run 3,
   carried). Such an attempt ends indeterminate with its calls unjournaled
   and its reservation, if one stands, unreleased. A run directory that
   stands without a readable reservation is a failure, not absence.
7. **A driver's record is identified by its site label**, the last written
   for its site; real labels are unique within an attempt.
8. **A record is written only under a lease**; a driver whose spawn fails
   after its record was written keeps a Settling record, which keeps a
   restart from recovering the attempt. Unsynced spawn recording refuses
   even an unstaked site (INFO).
9. **A refused checkpoint's CC2 cause** still ships without its field
   (operator ruling of 2026-10-20, carried to seat-record v7, #812).
10. **One transaction per call.** Both paths now append each settled
    checkpoint in its own compare-and-append transaction, where the
    running engine's used one chain transaction per broker; a ledger is
    bounded at 4,096 calls. brokkr-store has no expected-head chain append,
    and adding one is outside this unit.

The later-Settling panel half of `a_later_driver_still_settling_…` is
synthetic (records written as the door writes them); the real panel's door
and barrier are bound by `a_real_panels_members_are_recorded_and_recovered_from_their_heads`.
Observed by run 2's review, no fix asked and none made: a peer release
after a certified end can make the local release report a standing lease
that is already released, and the adopted door's `Lost` text is imprecise.
