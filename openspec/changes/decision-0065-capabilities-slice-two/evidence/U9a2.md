## What doctor reads, and through what

The compile fence is untouched: `Authority::load` is `validated` followed
by the realm-wide loop, and a compile still calls `load`.
`Authority::survey` is `validated` alone, typed as a compile refusal.

`brokkr_runtime::bundle::survey` composes the bundle as `compile_for` does,
reads its binding minimum, then its table, and, under `Authority::survey`
with that minimum, runs the compile's own `survey::seat`, `Seated::settle`
and `Seated::wrap` with `Refusals::Survey`. A compile runs the same three
with `Refusals::Compile`. Its `Survey` carries the minimum, each executable
site's `SiteCapabilities` at its compiled address, each refused site's
`CompileError`, each reached site's per-serving SI2 verdicts, and the
first failure no site owns. Doctor surveys only under a map it read.

For each valid `mcp` grant, read alone through `Authority::survey`, doctor
prints four warnings after the grant's unchanged refusal line: its facts,
its egress against the survey's minimum (named as the bundle's or the
default) by the compile's own comparison (`capabilities::admitted`,
`Below`), MB2's static eligibility with MB3's installation layout, and what
stays UNMEASURED. Then one line per serving of each planned site, with
U1g1's verdict; one line per refused site, with the compile's refusal and
each reached serving's verdict; and the survey's own failure, if any.

## Readings and residuals

No suppression was added. The survey reads the minimum before the table,
where a compile reads the machine, the dialect prompts and the undeclared
dialect refusal first, so a minimum that parses is compared even where an
earlier lint would stop a compile; that lint is then the survey's `failed`.

(the minimum unread) even if an earlier lint would also fail, while a
compile names the earlier one; doctor's egress line says the minimum could
not be read either way. A refusal kept under the dialect wrapper's
relocated address carries the words the capability pass wrote before the
wrapper ran (R1), as the compile's own refusal would.
