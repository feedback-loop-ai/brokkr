## Credential layers

dsh-credentials-local 0.1.5-rc.2 resolves a key from four layers, in order:
the process environment, `$DSH_HOME/.credentials.yaml`, `<cwd>/.env`, and
`$DSH_HOME/.env` (U0c). The engine-only home is created empty, so layers 2
and 4 do not exist in it, and the engine stages no key file there. Layer 3
is the seat's worktree, the child's cwd. `DshHome::stage` refuses before it
stages anything if `<workdir>/.env` exists in any form, a dangling link
included, or cannot be checked (a workdir that is a file, for example). That
closes the layer as it stands at launch. It does not close a `.env` the seat
creates while it runs (L1 below). Without an intent the operator's home is
served as before, with all four layers available.

## LOW findings and limits

L1 (credentials, LOW). A `.env` the seat creates in its worktree after
launch is not refused, despite the launch-time refusal. dsh would read it
as credential layer 3 on a later request. Closing it needs the seat's box or
dsh itself, which is outside this row's files.

L2 (placement, LOW, partly repaired). Homes now sit under
`$HOME/.local/state/brokkr/dsh-homes`. U0 and U0c staged theirs under
`/var/tmp/s2-u0c/dsh/home-engine-<family>`. Node's ancestor lookup from a
staged profile walks `<HOME>/.local/state/brokkr/dsh-homes`,
`<HOME>/.local/state/brokkr`, `<HOME>/.local/state`, `<HOME>/.local`,
`<HOME>`, the directory above `HOME` and `/`. U0 and U0c walked
`/var/tmp/s2-u0c/dsh`, `/var/tmp/s2-u0c`, `/var/tmp`, `/var` and `/`.
`composite.rs`'s `node_modules_paths` mirrors that lookup. A `node_modules`
in any of those `HOME` ancestors, `~/node_modules` for example, is therefore
reachable from the staged profile, as it already is from the operator's own
home. No dsh run has been made with a home in this placement.

L3 (retention, LOW, repaired for refusals). A home whose launch was served
is retained for its transcript and never reaped. One accumulates per served
launch that carries an intent.

L4 (environment, LOW). Inherited non-key environment, such as `NODE_PATH`
and `NODE_OPTIONS`, reaches the child unchanged and is unmeasured.

L5 (qualification binding, LOW). `DSH_ROUTES` is not bound to the measured
binary, version, endpoint or `apiKeyEnv`. U1e owns the dsh declarations.

L7 (resume, LOW). `Engine { rejoins: true }` stages a fresh home, so it can
never rejoin. An offered root's recorded home is refused as
`instance-changed`. No dsh resume was measured, so refusal and cold
replacement are all this unit claims.

The binding refusal applies only to engine-only homes, as commissioned. An
operator-home launch with a declared `DSH_HOME` binding behaves as on main.
That is a follow-up, not this unit's change.

The production placement of route rows in the seat's overlay was not run by
U0c, which placed them in the home's profile layer. Both compose after the
shipped bundles, and neither is ambient. The scaffold is produced by the
seat's own first launch, whereas U0c's homes were scaffolded by its
`--dump-config` pre-check. The `spark` route is admitted on the controller's
ruling, since U0 ran `spark-glm`. U0 and U0c are Linux cold evidence; nothing
is claimed for macOS.
