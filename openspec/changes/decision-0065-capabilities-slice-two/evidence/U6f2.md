## The one production change

The unit's one permitted production change (operator ruling 2026-10-14) is in
`crates/brokkr-cli/src/broker/session/stderr.rs`, the existing private child
of `session.rs`. `Voice` gains a fixed label, empty for the broker's own
voice. `Drain::new` builds its voice with `SERVER`, `"MCP server stderr: "`,
so every piece of every line kept of the server's standard error, a marker in
a line's place included, begins with it. `cut` puts the label at the head of
each piece and cuts the line's text at its safe points into at most
`ATOMIC - 1 - SERVER.len()` bytes, so the label and newline count inside each
piece's 512 bytes. Each piece is guarded as written, label included, after
the same masking and sanitization as before. A line that no labelled piece
can cut safely still falls back to its labelled marker, and to nothing where
that is refused too. The broker's own last line (`last`, `UNREPORTED`) stays
unlabelled. Exit status stays authoritative. `SERVER` is
`pub(in crate::broker)` so that the masking unit tests can label their
expected tails by it. No other production file moved: `session.rs`,
`lifetime.rs` and every other production file keep their bytes. The public
API, witness inputs and compose inputs do not change.

The label's text is pinned once in its module's tests
(`every_piece_of_a_servers_line_is_labelled_within_its_bound_and_the_brokers_own_never`)
and once in the public suite (`capability_broker/protocol.rs`'s `SERVER`,
which `relayed` applies to expected tails). Expectations that read a server's
standard error were updated to the labelled form, in eight tests of
`broker/rpc/tests/masking.rs`, two of `capability_broker/protocol.rs` and
eight of `capability_broker/serving.rs`. Both cases of the one-write cut test
in `serving.rs` move their leading spaces by the label's 19 bytes so that the
first labelled piece still straddles the bound number. The tail-bound test
in `masking.rs` sizes its lines to one labelled piece.

## Residuals and pending

The tree a killed process leaves is a residual whose removal depends on
the next start. A broker killed outright, or an observer the broker kills
at its deadline, its cancellation or its own kill, leaves its server
identity tree in the TMPDIR it ran with. The next `brokkr run`, `resume` or
`rerun` started with that TMPDIR reaps it, as shown above. Where no start
follows, or one starts with another TMPDIR, the tree stays.

The #403 limits stand, and none is claimed fixed. Every ownership
assertion here is a process-group and session observation and no cgroup
guarantee. A descendant that leaves the group is caught only by #403's
subreaper accounting, which parks the attempt as unresolved, as the restart
test shows. Bubblewrap's cgroup namespace is `--unshare-cgroup-try`.

One ending is reported as observed, not changed. When the harness ends its
input while a call is active and the server then exits, the broker exits 0
and the ledger stays unclosed, the call begun (`Invalid::Lifecycle`). That
is the existing rule that a harness which has closed its end keeps its
ending. CR4 judges the ledger, not that exit code.

A call's own 120 s deadline is not exercised through the public command,
because it outlasts the suite's 90 s bound. Unit tests in `broker/rpc`
cover it.
